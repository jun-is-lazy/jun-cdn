same drawn family throughout (grandma about 70, her daughter about 42, grandson about 8), so no stranger is on screen.

reel's clips (480p). ll08 to ll30 are new: a keyframe on Seedream 5.0 Pro, then Seedance 2.5 at 720p, 4 s, all on the
